Built to adapt

Not a template you have to squeeze into.

Most planners start by deciding how many children you have, what subjects they study, and what a school week should look like. Homeschoolable starts with the opposite assumption: you decide.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Add the children you need

Build the planner around one learner or several. Each child can have their own subjects, workload, and colour-coded schedule.

📚

Choose your own subjects

Add math, language arts, science, Bible, history, French, electives, therapies, projects, or whatever belongs in your homeschool.

🕘

Edit the school day

Change times, reorder learning blocks, add breaks, remove blocks, or create different rhythms for different days.

🗓️

Adjust the school week

Use five days, four days, alternating schedules, catch-up Fridays, co-op days, appointments, or whatever works for your family.

📆

Shape the school year

Set your start date, breaks, holidays, school weeks, and yearly goals instead of adapting your family to someone else's calendar.

✅

Track lessons and progress

Set course targets, record completed lessons, and see whether each subject is on pace over the year.

Made for real life

Because homeschool plans change.

A child finishes math early. A doctor's appointment moves science. A curriculum takes longer than expected. You decide to take Friday off and make it up next week.

The planner is designed to be changed as often as the plan changes. It is a working tool, not a form you fill out once and then feel guilty about ignoring.
